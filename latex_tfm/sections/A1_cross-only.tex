\section{Analysis fitting only cross-spectra}
\label{sec:appendix_1}

This appendix presents the results of the global power-law and bin-to-bin fitting analyses when only cross-spectra are used, i.e.\ excluding all auto-spectra from the likelihood. This configuration eliminates any noise bias contribution to the fitted signal, since cross-spectra between independent detector splits are unaffected by the noise auto-correlation. It therefore serves as a robustness check on the baseline results presented in Section~\ref{sec:results}, and on the goodness of fit discussed in relation to Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix_3}.

\subsection{Power-law fitting}

The cross-spectra-only posterior constraints over $20 \leq \ell \leq 200$ are reported in Table~\ref{tab:fit_all_masks_cross_only_ell20-200} and should be compared directly with the baseline auto+cross results in Table~\ref{tab:fit_all_masks_all_pairs_ell20-200}. The two sets of results are in excellent agreement. All parameter estimates are consistent within $1$-$2\sigma$ across all masks, dataset combinations, and polarization modes. The synchrotron amplitudes $A_s$, frequency spectral indices $\beta_s$, angular spectral indices $\alpha_s$, and all dust parameters ($A_d$, $\alpha_d$, $\beta_d$) show no statistically significant shift when the auto-spectra are removed. Similarly, the correlation coefficient $\rho$ remains stable between the two configurations. This confirms that the baseline results are not driven by noise bias in the auto-spectra, and that the foreground model is robustly constrained by the cross-spectra alone.

The main observable difference is in the parameter uncertainties. Removing the auto-spectra reduces the number of spectra accounted for the fit from 105 to 91, which results in a systematic increase in the posterior widths. For the QJ+WP+Pl dataset, the $1\sigma$ uncertainties on $A_s^{EE}$ and $\alpha_s^{EE}$ increase by roughly $10$-$15\%$, while the BB uncertainties are similarly affected. The increase is larger for $\beta_s^{BB}$ in the WP+Pl case, where the auto-spectra contribute meaningfully to breaking the synchrotron-noise degeneracy at low frequencies. Overall, the constraining power remains largely unaffected.

\paragraph{}

The reduced $\chi^2$ values (Table~\ref{tab:chi2_all}, Appendix~\ref{sec:appendix_3}) improve, but not significantly when auto-spectra are not accounted. For the EE mode with QJ+WP+Pl, $\chi^2_{\rm red}$ decreases by a factor $\sim1.2$-$1.4$ in the cross-only configuration. For the BB mode there is also an improvement, however, only by a factor $\sim1.1$-$1.2$. The EE values remain elevated even in the cross-only case, particularly for the $|b|>10^\circ$ mask, which suggests that the residual mismatch reflects genuine limitations of the single power-law model at low latitudes rather than noise misestimation in the auto-spectra. The BB $\chi^2_{\rm red}$ values are consistently closer to unity in both configurations, indicating that the model captures the BB spectra more accurately, consistent with the simpler signal structure of the B modes, but also as a consequence of the larger uncertainties present in the BB power spectra.

\subsection{Bin-to-bin fitting}

The cross-spectra-only bin-to-bin results are reported in Tables~\ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut10_cross_only}, \ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut15_cross_only} and \ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut20_cross_only}, and should be compared with the all-pairs results in Tables~\ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut10_all_pairs}, \ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut15_all_pairs} and \ref{tab:bin_to_bin_galcut20_all_pairs} respectively. The two sets of results are in excellent agreement across all bins, masks, and polarization modes. The per-bin $\beta_s$ posteriors cluster around $-3.0$ to $-3.3$ in both configurations, with no systematic shift when the auto-spectra are removed. The dust spectral index $\beta_d$ remains stable around $1.53$ in both EE and BB throughout the full multipole range. The correlation coefficient $\rho$ shows the same qualitative behaviour as in the all-pairs case, presenting small and positive EE values at low $\ell$ decreasing with Galactic latitude cut, and noisier BB estimates consistent with zero to $\sim 0.1$ at all scales. All per-bin estimates are consistent within $1$-$2\sigma$ between the two configurations.
